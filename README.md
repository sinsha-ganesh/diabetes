# diabetes

Diabetes is a chronic disease that occurs when the body cannot effectively regulate blood glucose (sugar) levels. It is one of the leading health concerns worldwide, affecting millions of people and increasing the risk of serious complications such as heart disease, kidney failure, nerve damage, and vision problems. Early detection and proper management of diabetes are essential for improving patient outcomes and reducing healthcare costs.

With the rapid growth of healthcare data and advancements in machine learning, predictive models can assist in identifying individuals who are at risk of developing diabetes. By analyzing various medical parameters such as glucose levels, blood pressure, body mass index (BMI), insulin levels, age, and other health indicators, machine learning algorithms can provide accurate predictions and support clinical decision-making.

This project focuses on developing a Diabetes Prediction System using machine learning techniques. The system aims to analyze patient data, identify patterns associated with diabetes, and predict the likelihood of the disease. The primary objective is to support early diagnosis, enhance preventive healthcare, and provide a reliable tool for healthcare professionals and individuals to assess diabetes risk effectively.
